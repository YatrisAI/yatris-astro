Reservations can now be placed on a site (YatrisCMS#421). `<ReservationEmbed setupKey="…" />` (`@yatris/astro/ReservationEmbed.astro`) renders an accessible iframe to the Yatris-hosted booking page on `book.yatris.jp`, with loading, unavailable and retry states and an always-visible direct link; an unpaired site shows the unavailable state, never a fake booking. The reservation contract gains the theme contract (colours, font stacks, spacing, radius; `validateTheme`, `encodeTheme`, `decodeTheme`, fixtures in `fixtures/theme.json`) and the versioned `postMessage` protocol (`parseBookingMessage`, `bookingMessage`, URL builders), both documented for the PHP twin. `@yatris/astro/booking/client` (`mountBooking`) is the framework-free time-slot booking flow shared by the hosted page and the new synthetic preview: `astro dev` with `YATRIS_RESERVATIONS_PREVIEW=1` runs the local declaration against clearly labelled synthetic hosts and availability and sends nothing, and `astro build` refuses the flag. `yatris reservations validate [<path>…]` checks declarations offline. `YATRIS_BOOKING_ORIGIN` points embeds at a local booking host during development.
