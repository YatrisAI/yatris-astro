Booking page polish for the smallest phones: the 空席表 week range sits on its own line above 前の7日 / 次の7日, step labels sit under their numbers instead of being cut to 「日時を…」, Turnstile switches to its compact size when the page is narrower than 300px, 「前へ」「次へ」 become arrows below 280px, the next-available button wraps after the date, and Japanese text breaks between phrases everywhere.
