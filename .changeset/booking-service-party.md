The booking UI (`@yatris/astro/booking/client`) now draws the business flows (YatrisCMS#426). A `business` + `service` setup goes service → variant (each with its own duration) → practitioner preference (「指定しない」 leaves `practitionerKey` to the server) → date and time → details → review → outcome; a `business` + `party` setup asks the party size within `minSize`…`maxSize` first and shows the dining duration, never tables or pools. Both keep everything the time-slot flow guarantees (time zones, holds, idempotent submit, Turnstile, truthful outcomes, the postMessage protocol, theme and accessibility), feed `booking.service_key`, `booking.variant_key` and `booking.party_size` to question conditions as the server evaluates them, and send the visitor back to the right step with a Japanese notice when the server answers `invalid_selection`. New exports: `flowOf`, `stepsOf`, `selectionProblem`, `durationOf`, `contextValuesOf`, `keptSelection`, `sensitiveKeys`, `redactedBooking`. A live mount takes an optional `siteOrigin`, so a consent question's privacy link opens on the Website rather than the booking host (default: the embed's parent origin). The synthetic preview now covers service and party declarations too, with fake practitioner names, durations per selection inside the opening periods, and sensitive answers redacted from its log.
